# Photographs on this website

The shop portrait and the 19 product photos in `public/images/cycles/` were
supplied by the shop for use on the website. The product photos appear in the
cycle catalogue and on their detail pages. Some were cropped to remove excess
blank space and converted to WebP for faster loading.

Brand names and trademarks visible in the photos belong to their respective
owners. A photo does not confirm current stock, specifications or price;
customers should contact the shop to confirm those details.
