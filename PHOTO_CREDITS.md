# Photographs on this website

The shop team banner (with a mobile crop) and the 24 product photos in
`public/images/cycles/` numbered `cycle-01`–`cycle-24` were supplied by the shop
for use on the website. The product photos appear in the cycle catalogue and on
their detail pages. Some were cropped to remove excess blank space and converted
to WebP for faster loading.

The Stryder electric cycle photograph (`cycle-24.jpg`) is taken from the
supplier's listing shared by the owner; it is not a shop photograph.

## Studio renders added in November 2026 (`cycle-25`–`cycle-39`)

The owner supplied more cycle pictures: photographs taken on the shop floor and
render-style posters published by the Gang brand (SK Bikes). For each of those
cycles a studio picture was prepared from the supplied picture so that the
catalogue looks consistent: the shop floor, cardboard, plastic wrapping, people,
poster lettering and scenery were removed, and the bicycle was placed on a plain
neutral background. Nothing about the cycle itself was redesigned — colours,
decals, basket, carrier, backrest and training wheels stay as they appear in the
supplied picture — and each cycle has its own picture; none is shared between two
products.

A one-page review sheet of the fifteen new pictures is kept at
`docs/new-cycles-review.jpg` so the owner can check each cycle against the
counter.

These pictures are AI-cleaned renders, not photographs taken in the shop. They
are labelled `photoKind: 'catalogue'` on the website, so each product page tells
the customer it is a catalogue picture rather than the exact bicycle standing on
the floor. A render does not confirm current stock, specifications or price;
customers should contact the shop to confirm those details.

Brand names and trademarks visible in the photos belong to their respective
owners. (The Minnie kids cycle is a shop children's cycle with character
graphics; the character artwork belongs to its owner.)

## The homepage shop film

`public/video/hero-640.mp4` and `public/video/hero-960.mp4` are re-encodes of
`CEMK.mp4`, uploaded by the shop owner to their own repository
(<https://github.com/authorsauravkushwaha/ai_automation>). It was recorded inside
Kanchan Cycle Stores.

The web copies keep the section from 7 s to 71.5 s of the original: the opening
brand slide, a 1.5 s black gap at 4.8 s and the silent trailing tail are cut, and
the footage is re-encoded at web bitrates. The shop footage itself is unaltered.

`public/video/hero-poster.webp` is the first frame of that section, used as the
poster so the hero is never an empty rectangle while the film loads.

The owner's photograph (`public/images/kanchan-team-hero.*`) is used exactly as
supplied. Only its frame on the homepage is new.
