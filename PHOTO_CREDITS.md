# Photographs on this website

The shop team banner (with a mobile crop) and the 23 product photos in `public/images/cycles/` were
supplied by the shop for use on the website. The product photos appear in the
cycle catalogue and on their detail pages. Some were cropped to remove excess
blank space and converted to WebP for faster loading.

The Stryder electric cycle photograph (`cycle-24.jpg`) is taken from the
supplier's listing shared by the owner; it is not a shop photograph. A photo
does not confirm current stock, specifications or price; customers should
contact the shop to confirm those details.

owners. A photo does not confirm current stock, specifications or price;
